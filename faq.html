Frequently asked questions

Short answers for investors, corporate venture teams and legal advisers.

What does technical due diligence cover?

An independent assessment of a company's core technology: whether the key claims are credible, how much further the technology can be developed, and what the main technical, manufacturing and supply-chain risks are. Claims are checked against physical limits and the published literature.

How long does it take?

A red-flag review typically takes 2 working days. A full technical due diligence typically takes 5–10 working days, depending on scope and access to the data room and the team.

Which technologies do you assess?

Photonics and flat optics (metasurfaces, metalenses, integrated photonics), quantum and precision sensing, MEMS and microsystems, space and free-space optical communications, photonic AI hardware and optical computing, and fibre and infrastructure sensing.

What do we receive?

A short summary of findings and our technical verdict, a table of the main claims and our assessment of each, estimated performance limits, a ranked list of technical risks with suggested tests, and questions for the founders. Calculations and sources are provided for all key figures.

Is this investment advice?

No. Our reports give technical opinion only and are not investment, financial or legal advice. Investment decisions remain with the client. Stellar Optics is independent of the University of Southampton.

Is the work confidential?

Yes. All work is carried out under a non-disclosure agreement, and client and target information is never reused.

Which languages do you work in?

English and Chinese.

Do you carry out patent searches?

Yes. We carry out prior-art searches, patent landscapes and technical analysis of patent claims, either on their own or as part of a due diligence.

Do you offer short consultations?

Yes. We provide focused technical consultations on a specific technology in photonics, sensing and microsystems.
