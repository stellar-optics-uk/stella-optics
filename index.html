What we assess

Our assessment starts with the technology itself and the physics behind it.

1

Feasibility

We check the key performance claims against fundamental physical limits and the published state of the art.

2

Headroom

We estimate how far the technology can realistically be improved, and how close the team already is to that limit.

3

Risk

We identify the technical, manufacturing and supply-chain risks that matter most, and suggest practical tests to resolve them early.

How we work

  • Claims are reviewed against physical principles and the published literature.
  • Key figures are recalculated or simulated independently, rather than taken from company materials.
  • The written report covers a summary and recommendation, an assessment of each main claim, performance limits, ranked technical risks with suggested tests, and questions for the founders, with calculations and sources for all key figures.
  • All work is under a non-disclosure agreement; client information is not used for any other purpose.
  • Each project is checked for export-control and national-security issues before work starts.
